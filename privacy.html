Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Zooming Sparks ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Harry Duarte, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth, we access only the scopes you explicitly authorize, such as your TikTok Open ID, display name, profile avatar, and content publishing permissions, along with authentication tokens.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to authenticate your TikTok account, execute user-initiated actions (such as uploading or publishing videos), and maintain the functionality of Zooming Sparks.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is only transmitted to TikTok's official APIs to fulfill your requests or as required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and user data are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored securely only for as long as needed to provide the service.

5. User Rights and Data Deletion

You can revoke Zooming Sparks' access at any time in the TikTok app under Profile > Menu > Settings and Privacy > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions. To request full deletion of any stored data, email tu-correo@ejemplo.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we will delete your records within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Zooming Sparks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: tu-correo@ejemplo.com
